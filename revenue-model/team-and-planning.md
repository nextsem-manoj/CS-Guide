# Nextsem Academy: Team Operations & Revenue Planning

This guide outlines the execution strategy for scaling Nextsem Academy to support a monthly budget of **Rs. 65,000** (Rs. 40,000 for a team of 4 + Rs. 25,000 for the project lead) using a combination of AdMob and AdSense ad formats.

---

## 1. Team Structure & Weekly Tasks
To maintain a high quality of content and sustain progress, the team of 4 is divided into specialized roles with clear weekly goals:

### Member 1: Curriculum & Content Writer
* **Salary:** Rs. 10,000/month
* **Responsibilities:** Researching and writing job-oriented, "Zero-to-Hero" MDX frontend course modules.
* **Weekly Goal:** Deliver **3 high-quality lessons/projects** (React, Tailwind, Next.js, Git) optimized for Google search query intent.

### Member 2: Visual Designer & illustrator
* **Salary:** Rs. 10,000/month
* **Responsibilities:** Creating diagrams, infographics, visual concept cards, and promo assets.
* **Weekly Goal:** Deliver **10 clean visual illustrations** to replace long blocks of text in lessons.

### Member 3: App Developer
* **Salary:** Rs. 10,000/month
* **Responsibilities:** Building coding playgrounds, drag-and-drop structures, compiling release builds, and resolving bugs.
* **Weekly Goal:** Maintain the simulator, integrate ad slots, sync Capacitor platforms, and verify build health.

### Member 4: Content Marketer & SEO Lead
* **Salary:** Rs. 10,000/month
* **Responsibilities:** Managing search engines SEO optimization for `nextsem.online`, running the YouTube channel, and writing LinkedIn "Build in Public" updates.
* **Weekly Goal:** Produce **2 YouTube tutorial videos/Shorts** and **3 LinkedIn posts** pointing back to the app.

---

## 2. Monetization Expansion Strategy

### High-Impact Ad Formats (AdMob Mobile)
1. **Option A (Interstitial Ads):** Triggers when users tap "Mark Lesson as Complete". This maximizes impressions without interrupting the reading flow.
2. **Option B (Dashboard Banners):** Passive adaptive banners displayed at the bottom of the course curriculum menu layout.
3. **Option C (Rewarded Video Ads):**
   * **Classroom Premium Badge:** Watch a rewarded video ad to display a premium icon next to the profile.
   * **Reference PDFs / Code Templates:** Prompt users to watch a rewarded video ad to download premium CSS cheat sheets, layouts, or source code templates.

---

## 3. Global Traffic Scaling

To reduce dependency on low-CPM traffic, target the English-speaking global market (US, UK, Europe, Australia) which offers up to **10x higher RPM rates** than local markets:

* **GitHub Open Source Integration:** Publish public code repositories for all tutorial projects. Link back to the app in the `README.md` to capture organic developer traffic.
* **Global SEO Keywords:** Optimize article titles and YouTube videos for global developer search intents (e.g., *"How to implement custom hooks in Next.js"*).
* **Metrics to Track Weekly:**
  * **Impression RPM:** Target a combined RPM of **$6.00+**.
  * **Daily Active Users (DAU):** Aim for a baseline target of **500 DAU**.
  * **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Monitor ad clicks on interstitials and rewards.
