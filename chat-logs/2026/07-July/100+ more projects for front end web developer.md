* **Website Hosting:** Your website `nextsem.online` is hosted on **Firebase Hosting**. It handles millions of requests per second with edge-caching, meaning 17,000+ page views is mathematically trivial for it.
* **App Architecture:** The mobile app is built as a hybrid Capacitor package. All HTML, CSS, and JS files are compiled and installed **locally** on the student's phone. This results in near-zero database load.

### 2. Google Ad Server Scaling (AdSense & AdMob)

### 3. Optimized Ad-Serving Code
Our custom components, specifically [AdSenseBanner.tsx](file:///c:/jbrs-landing-page/src/components/AdSenseBanner.tsx), were built from the ground up to prevent common scaling bottlenecks:
* **Ad-Blocker Protection:** If a user has an active ad-blocker or Google fails to serve an ad within **2 seconds**, the component automatically swaps the empty frame with a premium **static fallback banner** promoting Nextsem Academy. This keeps the layout beautiful, prevents broken margins, and drives students to other parts of the curriculum.
* **Responsive Visual Adaptation:** The ad banners dynamically detect and match the student's active theme (Sepia, Light, or Dark Mode). They adjust their background wraps to match, ensuring no jarring contrast or layout shifts (CLS) that could lead to Google policy warnings.
* **Preloaded Interstitials & Rewards:** High-yield ad formats (like rewarded videos and completion interstitials) are preloaded and prepared in the background before they are shown. This ensures that when a student taps "Mark Complete" or "Unlock Badge," the ad displays instantly with zero screen freeze.
