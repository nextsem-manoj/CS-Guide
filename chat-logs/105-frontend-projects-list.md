```mermaid
graph TD
    A["HTML5 & CSS3 Basics <br> (Start here)"] -->|Master Layouts| B["Vanilla JS & DOM Manipulation <br> (Learn logic)"]

    D -->|Deploy full web apps| E["Full-Scale Clones & PWAs <br> (Portfolio Ready!)"]
```
