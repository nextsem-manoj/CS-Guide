# 📄 MS Word Career Skills — Lesson Plan
**Target:** Job seekers & working professionals (18–40 yrs) who want to boost their career using Microsoft Word 365

---

## 🎯 Theme: "Office Ready — From Zero to Professional"
> Real job tasks. Real office scenarios. Real career impact.

**Core philosophy:** Every lesson is tied to an actual job task — writing a resume, formatting a report, drafting a business letter, collaborating with teammates in real time. No boring theory without purpose.

## 🎭 Content Style Rules (for all 60 lessons)

- ✅ **Every lesson opens with a real job story** ("Rahul was asked by his manager to...")
- ✅ **Hinglish explanations** for concepts that feel complex in English
- ✅ **Kid mode** = Office worker learning for the first time (analogies, step-by-step)
- ✅ **Pro mode** = IT professional / senior office manager wanting depth
- ✅ **Each lesson ends** with one interactive MCQ simulation question
- ✅ **Visual Demo widget** shows what the Word feature looks like in action
- ✅ **AI tip box** in every lesson: "🤖 Copilot Tip: You can also ask AI to do this for you..."
