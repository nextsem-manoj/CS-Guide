1. How Your Learning Progress Is Saved (Zero-Lag + Cloud Safe)

Why does my dashboard say "Saved Locally on Device"?

Tier 1: Device Memory (0ms Local-First)

Whenever you solve a coding lab, submit a GitHub link, or update your social profiles, the app saves it immediately into your device's browser memory (localStorage).

  • ✓Zero Wait Time: No loading spinners or blank screens when opening your dashboard.
  • ✓100% Offline Ready: Continue coding and practicing even when traveling or without internet.
  • ✓Persistent: Closing the tab or restarting your PC does not delete your work.
Tier 2: Google Cloud Firestore (Background Backup)

When you sign in with your Google Account, a background worker silently mirrors your progress to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

  • ✓Multi-Device Sync: Solve Day 1 on your phone, then open Day 2 on your laptop seamlessly.
  • ✓Disaster Recovery: Even if you clear your browser cache, logging back in instantly restores everything.
  • ✓Permanent Security: Linked to your verified Google UID.

2. Why 360 Practical Days for a 3-Year BCA Degree?

Understanding the college semester academic timeline

The Academic Semester Math
3 Years = 6 Semesters × 60 Practical Lab Days = 360 Days

In a standard 6-month university semester, approximately 90 days are active teaching days (excluding examination weeks, admission cycles, and holiday breaks). Out of those 90 days, exactly 60 days are allocated to rigorous programming lab sessions.
